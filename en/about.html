About us

A venture builder that takes you all the way to a deal

Georgia is a small market with plenty of talent but limited access to capital. We address the latter: not training, but deal preparation and access to investors.

Why this is needed

  • Government programs provide training, but do not take equity or bring in private capital
  • International accelerators operate in Georgia under government contracts and leave when those contracts end
  • After the program, no one guides the team toward a funding round
  • Founders lack a network of international investors

How we make money

  • Corporate acceleration: scouting and pilots tailored to a company’s needs
  • Program fees for teams that do not give up equity
  • We invest alongside a group of private investors and receive equity on the same terms
  • Market entry: a package for teams from abroad testing the Georgian market over three months

Principles

Four rules we never compromise on.

Metrics, not words

Program entry and completion are measured in numbers: revenue, customers, and the sales funnel.

Rejections in writing

Every team we turn down receives a reason and a list of things to improve.

No promises of returns

We do not guarantee investment or sell “participation in a funding round.”

Public statistics

After each cohort, we publish how many teams completed the program, how many raised funding, and how many shut down.

Team

The board and mentor team are being assembled. Personal information is not published in this prototype: names, photos, and biographies will be added with each person’s consent.

Organization

I4 GROUP is a private venture builder working in partnership with government programs and corporations. Legal details and ownership structure are not displayed on the public version of the website.
