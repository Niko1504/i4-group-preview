I4 IRA — Investment Readiness Accelerator

12 weeks · Pre-seed and Seed · Tbilisi and online · applications open

IRA stands for Investment Readiness Accelerator. The name explains the purpose: your team refines its numbers, documents and pitch to the point where an investor is ready to consider a deal. Not a course — 12 weeks of work on your own project with mentors and a review by the investment committee.

Week-by-week program

  • Weeks 1–2 — assessment: market, product, team, finances
  • Weeks 3–4 — metrics: funnel, retention, acquisition cost
  • Weeks 5–6 — unit economics and a 24-month financial model
  • Weeks 7–8 — investor materials: deck, one-pager, 5- and 15-minute pitches
  • Weeks 9–10 — deal: valuation, SAFE, cap table, due diligence
  • Weeks 11–12 — meetings with funds, including international funds, and Demo Day

Who it is for

  • A working product and initial customers
  • Ready to enter international markets, not just Georgia
  • A team of at least two, with one full-time member
  • Goal: a $150–500 thousand round within 6 months after the program

What your team gets

  • Founder and investor mentors, not consultants
  • A review of your model by the investment committee
  • Meetings with funds and angels from the accelerator’s network
  • Deal document templates

Participation terms: a program fee or equity in the company after the round. We discuss the figures on a call and set them out in a contract.

How we take equity

Three participation options. Your team chooses before the program starts, and the terms are set out in a contract.

OPTION A · EQUITY

Equity for the program

No upfront cash payment. We earn from the growth in our equity’s value, so we stay with your team for 12 months after Demo Day.

  • priority engagement with our investors
  • support with fundraising and hiring
OPTION B · FEE

Fixed fee

For teams that do not want to dilute their equity at an early stage. The program and deal documents are provided for a fee, with no equity stake.

  • 3 months of support
  • access to the investor database and meetings with funds
OPTION C · ALONGSIDE ANGELS

Investment alongside angels

We join the round on the same terms as private investors: a standard SAFE, with no separate equity increase.

  • investment terms set out in a contract
  • a deal decision within 5 days

We discuss the equity stake and terms on a call and set them out in a contract. This is not a public offer.

I4 Landing — three months in Tbilisi

3 months · Series A–B teams · Tbilisi · by application

Test a new market without relocating the entire company. Your team comes to Tbilisi for three months: we handle the legal and operational side while you test demand with real customers. At the end, you receive a report on every meeting and decide whether to enter the country’s market.

Legal entity and finances

Company registration, a bank account, a tax model and bookkeeping for the first quarter.

Team and office

Help relocating key team members, a workspace in Tbilisi and access to the local community.

Pilots

Five targeted meetings with local customers and partners matched to your product, not general networking.

The package does not include relocating the entire team, investment in your project or guaranteed contracts. We are responsible for coordination and access to customers; your team decides whether to enter the market.
